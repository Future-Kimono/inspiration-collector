# Seeds — inspiration to explore, not rules to obey

**Created 7 August 2026, after Ladana pointed out the learning system was convergent.**

> *"My ideas are just inspiration. I don't want the output to only look like my ideas —
> there are thousands of creative options and I can only think of a few at a time."*

This file exists to hold Ladana's and James's own research **without it becoming law.**

---

## The distinction this whole file protects

There are three kinds of input to this system and they must never be treated the same way.

| Kind | Example | Effect | Narrows or widens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Constraint** | "No women's products." "Must be sublimatable." | Hard filter, permanent | Narrows — correctly |
| **Quality floor** | "This looks cheap." | Raises the bar *within* a direction | Neither — lifts |
| **Seed** | "I found this Showa poster archive, it's amazing." | Adds somewhere new to look | **Widens** |

**A seed never becomes a filter.** Finding something you love does not mean everything must
now look like it. It means there is one more door worth opening.

The failure mode this prevents: Ladana shares five references she loves, the collector reads
them as a specification, and every future run returns variations on those five things. That
is a search engine for her existing taste, not a research tool.

---

## How a seed is used

When a run happens, seeds are **prompts to explore**, weighted alongside — never instead of —
the collector's own hunting:

1. Read the unexplored seeds below.
2. Pick one or two and actually chase them, properly, not as a token gesture.
3. Report what came back, **including when a seed turned out to be a dead end** — that is
   just as useful and it stops the same door being opened twice.
4. Mark the seed `explored` or `exhausted` with a note.

**A seed being productive does not narrow the next run toward it.** It just means that door
stays open. A seed being a dead end closes only that door, not the neighbourhood.

## Adding a seed

Ladana or James says it in chat — a link, a screenshot, a description, a half-formed thought.
It gets added below verbatim, in their words, with no interpretation layered on top.

**Say it loosely.** "There's something in old Japanese matchbox labels" is a better seed than
a precise specification, because it leaves room for the collector to find things neither of
you had pictured.

### S11 in Ladana's words

> *"I like the idea of using the Chinese New Year animal as inspiration, as last year we did
> the product drop 'Serpent Core' in 2025 which was the year of the snake. So because this
> year is year of the horse we can lean into this as inspiration — but then I don't want you
> to go ahead and base all your research into looking for things related to horses. This is
> just one creative direction."*

**How to use it, and how not to.**

✅ Let it colour a slice of a run. Horses in ukiyo-e are already a rich seam — Yoshitoshi's
*Horse and Rider* was one of the strongest finds on the current board and was found before
this seed existed. Armour, tack, motion, mane as texture, the horse as a silhouette. Racing
and equestrian graphic traditions. The horse in Japanese and Chinese art. Speed and movement
as an idea rather than as a picture of an animal.

❌ Do **not** turn a run into a horse hunt. If a board comes back and most of it is horses,
that is a failure, not a success. There is also a real precedent worth noticing: *Serpent Core*
is not a picture of a snake — it is a snake rendered in cyan crosshatch with chromatic
aberration and oversized cropped script. **The zodiac gave it a name and a spine, not a
subject.** That is the level to work at.

## The board is a parts bin, not a shortlist

**Added 7 August 2026 — this is what the whole board is for.**

> *"If I had two shirts, and on one I liked the colours but hated the print, and on the other
> I liked the print but hated the colours — I could take the colours from one and the print
> from the other and combine them. That's the point of the board."*
> *(A top-level basic example. It is not just prints and colours — it could be the type, the
> layout, the texture, anything.)*

So a find is **not a proposal.** Nobody is being asked *"should we make this?"* They are being
asked *"is there anything in here worth taking?"*

What follows from that:

- **A find can be worth keeping for one element while the rest is bad.** A jersey with a
  terrible graphic but an unusual, memorable palette is a legitimate keeper — *as a palette*.
  That is exactly why every find is filed under one ingredient bucket.
- **When a find is kept, the useful question is which ingredient earned it.** If Ladana keeps
  something, note *what part* she is keeping where it is clear, or ask. "Kept for the palette,
  not the print" is far more useful than a bare star.
- **A star is not endorsement of the whole object**, and this should never be inferred. It
  means one or more elements are worth having in the parts bin.
- **The design work is combination.** Direction sheets are where colour from one find, layout
  from a second and type from a third get assembled. The board's job is to make sure there are
  enough good parts on the bench to combine — which is another reason breadth beats
  convergence.

### An open question this raises

The premium bar currently scores a find **holistically** — 4 of 6 or it does not reach the
curated tier. Under parts-bin logic that may be wrong: a find that scores 2/6 overall but has
one outstanding element could be more useful than a find that scores 5/6 and is merely tidy.

**Not changed unilaterally.** Worth deciding together — flagged here rather than acted on.

Status values: `unexplored` · `partly explored` · `explored` · `exhausted`

**Exhausted means the door was opened and there was nothing behind it** — not that it was
bad. Record why, so nobody re-runs it.

## The exploration quota

**Roughly a third of every run must come from somewhere not yet established.** This is a hard
requirement, not a preference, and it is what stops the tool converging.

Concretely, per run:

- **~2 finds must be wildcards** — deliberately outside the established pattern, marked
  `wildcard: true` on the board so they read differently.
- **~1 seed must be genuinely chased**, with what it produced written up.
- **The rest** may follow what has been working.

**Wildcards still have to clear the quality floor.** Exploration is not an excuse for junk —
it means looking somewhere new, not lowering the standard.

**Rejecting a wildcard costs nothing.** It never counts toward a standing filter and never
narrows a territory. That is the point: they are free to reject, so they can be genuinely
strange.

## Where this leaves the reject buttons

They still work, but their scope is deliberately limited:

- A reject means **"this execution is wrong"** — never "this whole direction is wrong."
- Three rejects sharing a reason raise the **floor** in that lane. They do not delete the lane.
- **Only Ladana or James saying so in words** can close a direction entirely. A button click
  can never do that.

If the collector ever writes a rule broader than the evidence supports, that is a bug. Say so
and it gets narrowed.

## Mechanics extracted from Ladana's own research — 7 Aug 2026

Read off her four references rather than described in the abstract. **These are
ingredients to combine, not a look to reproduce.**

| Mechanic | Where it came from | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Three-density camo** | Venum desert rash guard | One motif in three treatments — solid fill, hatched fill, speckled distress. This is why it reads expensive rather than like stock camo |
| **No black at all** | Same | Warm neutrals only. Rare in a category that defaults to black, and an easy way to stand out on a mat |
| **Technical micro-type** | Same | `05`, `FW3`, boxed monograms as texture. Already native to FK — Serpent Core's 6pt footer |
| **Gold as line, not fill** | UFC 288 Champ **and** Venum Matupa | Traces panel seams instead of filling shapes. **Appears in two of her four references — the strongest repeated signal in her set** |
| **Name as hero** | UFC 288 Champ | Personalisation running the full spine. FK already sells name customisation as a small add-on; this makes it the whole design |
| **Tonal on tonal** | UFC red shorts, Venum Matupa | Reads as texture at distance, resolves as pattern up close. Works in saturated colour, not only black |
| **Animal print as texture** | Venum Matupa | Snake as an all-over surface rather than an illustrated creature — the opposite of how FK has used the serpent so far |
| **Hero silhouette** | Met shaffrons | A shape that survives being reduced to one colour. Ladana's read: stencil outline on a back panel |

### The horse, restated after seeing her reference

Ladana's read on the shaffron was **a stencil outline on a back panel**. That is the right
instinct and it connects to something already proven on this board: the Yoshitoshi finds all
work by putting one hero on a dark ground with everything else quiet. **A shaffron is a hero
shape.**

It is also Year of the Horse *without being a picture of a horse* — the same move as Serpent
Core being a snake in cyan crosshatch rather than a snake. Her own phrasing for the direction:
*"the horse becomes the hero, not the rider."*

### ⚠️ The balance risk she spotted herself

Three of her four references are Venum/UFC product photography. Weighted naively, the board
becomes a Venum catalogue — convergence in a new form. **The shaffron is the counterweight and
it is the one that is not a garment at all.** Hold that balance; do not chase the easy wins.
