# What this tool is

**Read this first. It overrules everything else in this repo.** If any other file
contradicts it, this file wins and the other file is wrong.

---

## The one-sentence version

**A mood board for Future Kimonos rash guard design, filled automatically instead of by hand.**

Ladana and James used to open Pinterest and Google and search for design inspiration
themselves. This tool does that research for them and puts everything in one place.

**Its job is to save them time.** That is the whole point.

---

## The working process

```
1. Ladana clicks run
        ↓
2. The collector searches, using SOURCES.md and SEEDS.md as guidance
        ↓
3. 20–30 new findings appear on the board
        ↓
4. Ladana and James review — ★ keep, or ✕ cut with a reason
        ↓
5. Those marks teach the collector what good looks like
        ↓
6. The next run is better
```

That is it. There is nothing else to understand.

## What a star means

**"I will keep this."** Either the whole thing, or one element of it — the palette, the print,
the type, the layout, anything.

It comes with a note, and the note has two jobs:

1. **For Ladana and James to read** when they come back to the board later.
2. **To teach the collector** what is being approved and why — building a knowledge base of
   what good output looks like.

## What a cut means

**"Do not show me this kind of thing again."** The reason attached to it is the instruction.
Cuts train the collector so it stops producing that sort of output.

## Why the guidance files exist

If you ask for 30 rash guard ideas with no constraints, the possibilities are unlimited and
the result is what came back on the very first run: **terrible.** All 12 finds were rejected.

Everything in `SOURCES.md` and `SEEDS.md` exists to narrow the search and point it at the
right places. It is guidance for *where to look*, not a specification for what to find.

- **`SOURCES.md`** — where to look. Brands, methods, archives.
- **`SEEDS.md`** — creative directions to explore. **These widen the search; they never
  restrict it to themselves.**
- **`REJECTS.md`** — what has already been ruled out, and why.
- **`BENCHMARK.md`** — the standard of craft, using Future Kimonos' own designs.

---

## Rules for whoever maintains this

**Keep it simple.** The tool grew a two-tier "curated vs product sweep" split that Ladana did
not ask for and did not want. It was removed on 7 August 2026. **One board, one flat list of
findings.** If a new structure is proposed, it has to earn its place by making the review
faster — not by being tidier to describe.

**Every finding is the same kind of thing:** something that might spark a design idea. A
museum print, a competitor's rash guard, a colour palette and a piece of lettering all sit on
the same board with the same buttons.

**The output is inspiration, not proposals.** Nobody is being asked *"should we make this?"*
They are being asked *"is there anything in here worth taking?"* Findings get combined —
colour from one, print from another, type from a third.

**20–30 findings per run.** Enough to be worth reviewing, few enough to get through.

**Look at every image before filing it.** Never write a design note from a product title.
