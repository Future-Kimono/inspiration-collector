# Inspiration Brief — Wednesday 5 August 2026 (Run 1, corrected rules)

First run under the no-gi-only rule. **36 finds on the board, all rash guards, shorts or spats.** No tees, caps, gis or general activewear. Macro trends and consumer voice live here in writing, not as pictures.

**Strongest signal:** a Reddit thread from someone starting a rash guard brand asked the community to choose between minimal-with-a-logo, comedic, or *"japanese style thats getting popular with loud design on the back"*. That third option is the exact territory Future Kimonos already owns — Akuma, Daruma, Neo Tokyo, Immortal Samurai, Serpent Core — described by a stranger as the thing that is currently getting popular. The brand's existing design language is not the problem. The absence of new product in it is.

## F-20260805-13 · The category's own customers describe FK's aesthetic as the rising one

**Trend** — In a thread asking what themes people want, the three options offered were minimal with a small logo, Japanese style with a loud back graphic described as "getting popular", or comedic. Separately, a buyer explicitly asked for "nothing loud or covered in massive designs… just a plain black one with maybe a small, minimal logo" and named the thing he was avoiding: slogans like "heel hooker".
**Evidence** — [Favorite rashguard designs?](https://old.reddit.com/r/bjj/search?q=rashguard+design&restrict_sr=on&sort=top&t=year) (21 comments), [The Rashguard Search](https://old.reddit.com/r/bjj/search?q=rashguard+design&restrict_sr=on&sort=top&t=year) (33 comments)
**Repetition** — first sighting
**Direction** — Japanese/loud-back: growing · plain black minimal: stable and clearly underserved
**Relevance** — Both poles have live demand and Future Kimonos only serves one. The plain-black-small-logo buyer is a real, vocal, price-sensitive segment the catalogue currently ignores entirely.
**Ingredient** — The loud-back structure itself: bare chest, single large graphic on the back. Already proven in-house on Serpent Core.
**Interpretation** — The evidence supports continuing the existing language rather than abandoning it for minimalism. But it also suggests one plain black SKU with a small logo would find buyers, and would happen to satisfy the internal taste preference without compromising the range.

## F-20260805-14 · Slow shipping is the category's open wound, and someone said the quiet part out loud

**Trend** — Four separate threads in the window complain about BJJ gear delivery. One buyer waited five weeks on a no-gi set shipping from Pakistan. Another documented pre-orders quoted at 3–4 weeks arriving in 6–8, and an "in stock" item cancelled after a week. Then this, verbatim: *"If any BJJ brand did overnight shipping and had cool designs, I'd probably buy their entire stock."*
**Evidence** — [BJJ Gear always takes 30 years to ship](https://old.reddit.com/r/bjj/search?q=rashguard+design&restrict_sr=on&sort=top&t=year) · [I'm Done Buying Gear From BJJ Fanatics](https://old.reddit.com/r/bjj/search?q=rashguard+design&restrict_sr=on&sort=top&t=year) (36 comments)
**Repetition** — first sighting, four independent threads
**Direction** — stable — this is a chronic category condition, not a moment
**Relevance** — This is the most commercially loaded finding in the run. Future Kimonos took reputational damage on exactly this issue, has since fixed it, and **already sells an Express Production Service SKU**. The category is loudly complaining about a problem the brand has already solved and is not talking about.
**Ingredient** — Not a design ingredient. A positioning one.
**Interpretation** — Marketing decision rather than a design decision, but it belongs in the seasonal brief: a drop whose headline is speed rather than graphics. Worth raising with James separately from the design work.

## F-20260805-15 · Everything ships as a kit, and one brand won't sell the halves

**Trend** — Not one rash guard in the entire scan shipped alone. Tatami publishes rash guard and shorts within seconds across Nomad, Contrast, Vortex and Butterfly. Shoyoroll paired Roan within thirteen seconds and Rali within thirty. Gold BJJ goes furthest and sells Topo only as a single kit SKU — you cannot buy the top on its own.
**Evidence** — [Gold BJJ Arctic Topo kit](https://goldbjj.com/products/arctic-topo-kit) · [Shoyoroll Roan shorts](https://shoyoroll.com/products/tvt001-8-shorts) · [Tatami Contrast shorts](https://www.tatamifightwear.com/products/ladies-contrast-high-cut-shorts-purple-black)
**Repetition** — first sighting, present at 4 of 4 brands with genuine drops
**Direction** — stable — an established norm
**Relevance** — Sets the design unit. One artwork must be authored to survive being cropped onto a leg panel with different geometry. Doubles basket size at no extra design cost.
**Ingredient** — Kit thinking. Tatami's Butterfly is the best study — a large repeating natural form survives the crop where a single centred graphic would be sliced.
**Interpretation** — Spec every new design as a two-piece from the outset. Gold's single-SKU model is worth testing on one drop.

## F-20260805-16 · Acid green is the cross-brand colour, not pink

**Trend** — High-visibility acid green appeared independently at two brands across four separate no-gi drops in seven weeks: Tatami's Technical and Vortex lines plus its ranked green, and Gold BJJ's Green Topo kit. Pink-and-black is more repeated overall but almost entirely within one brand's own range.
**Evidence** — [Tatami Technical green](https://www.tatamifightwear.com/products/technical-long-sleeve-rash-guard-green) · [Tatami Vortex green](https://www.tatamifightwear.com/products/vortex-rash-guard-green) · [Gold Green Topo kit](https://goldbjj.com/products/topo-no-gi-kit)
**Repetition** — first sighting, 4 drops, 2 brands
**Direction** — growing
**Relevance** — Cross-brand movement is a stronger signal than volume at one brand. Navy is the quieter version of the same story — Shoyoroll, Tatami and Gold all shipped navy independently, and it is far less crowded.
**Ingredient** — Acid green approx `#B4D330` against near-black. Hex read off product imagery, not harvested from metadata — treat as indicative.
**Interpretation** — Green is moving but risks arriving late. Navy is the safer cross-brand play and sits closer to the existing palette.

## F-20260805-17 · Two kinds of minimal that look identical and mean opposite things

**Trend** — Tatami's IBJJF Ranked line is unadorned because competition rules force a legible rank panel. Shoyoroll's Rali is unadorned because they chose it, shipping as a bare navy garment with a codename and no published colour metadata at all.
**Evidence** — [Tatami IBJJF Ranked green](https://www.tatamifightwear.com/products/ladies-ibjjf-ranked-long-sleeve-rash-guard-green) · [Shoyoroll Rali](https://shoyoroll.com/products/tvt001-3-ss-rash-guard)
**Repetition** — first sighting
**Direction** — both stable
**Relevance** — Directly relevant to the internal tension. If Future Kimonos wants a restrained SKU, ranked product is the route that requires no positioning argument — the rule justifies it. A chosen-minimal SKU has to earn its silence, which is much harder for a brand whose whole language is loud.
**Ingredient** — Anonymity as a device: codename, no explanatory copy, no published colour.
**Interpretation** — If a restraint direction is wanted, ranked is the low-risk door.

## F-20260805-18 · Range architecture beats new artwork

**Trend** — Three separate multiplier mechanics observed. Tatami's Contrast: one panel-block construction, fourteen colourways. Tatami's ranked line: six SKUs in one drop across two sleeve lengths, two ranks, men's and women's. Tatami's Nomad and Abstract: every colourway mirrored adult and kids in the same drop.
**Evidence** — [Contrast purple/black](https://www.tatamifightwear.com/products/ladies-contrast-long-sleeve-rash-guard-purple-black) · [Kids Nomad pink/black](https://www.tatamifightwear.com/products/kids-tatami-nomad-rash-guard-pink-black) · [Ranked green](https://www.tatamifightwear.com/products/ladies-ibjjf-ranked-short-sleeve-rash-guard-green)
**Repetition** — first sighting, three mechanics at one brand
**Direction** — stable
**Relevance** — This is the answer to the cadence problem. Future Kimonos has released no new design series since Serpent Core in February 2025 — eighteen months. Tatami is not out-designing them, it is out-structuring them. Ranked also guarantees repeat purchase as a customer promotes.
**Ingredient** — One construction, many colourways. One artwork, two age ranges.
**Interpretation** — The fastest route back to rhythm is not more artwork. It is extending existing artwork through colourways, sleeve lengths and kids sizing. Future Kimonos already runs kids sizing on most rash guards but does not merchandise it as a mirrored range.
