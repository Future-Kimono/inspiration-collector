# Inspiration Brief — Wednesday 5 August 2026

*First run. No prior ledger, so every finding below is a first sighting — direction calls are provisional until they repeat.*

**Strongest signal today:** BJJ's rash guard market has split into two clean poles — Shoyoroll's unbranded codename minimalism and Gold BJJ's commissioned-tattoo-artist maximalism — with very little surviving in the middle. Meanwhile the warm-brown palette that has taken over mainstream sportswear has not reached BJJ at all. That gap is the most interesting thing on the board, because it is cheap to test on a made-to-order model.

**BJJ relevance** — A genuine white space rather than a crowded trend. The risk is that BJJ's absence is a preference, not a lag: brown reads as "dirty" on a mat garment, which may be exactly why nobody has done it.

### F-20260805-11 · Halftone as an *engineering* signal, not a nostalgia one *(Lane C — pattern angle)*
