# The Design Inspiration Board — how to use it

**https://future-kimono.github.io/inspiration-collector/**

Open that link in any browser, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install, no password,
no account. Bookmark it.

---

## What it is

Every week, design research is gathered from across the internet — competitor kit,
motorsport graphics, museum print archives, streetwear — and the good bits are put on
one visual board. You look at it and say what you like and what you don't.

The point isn't just to browse. **What you mark teaches it.** The more you use it, the
closer the next board gets to what you actually want.

---

## Using it — three things

### 1. Look

The board is a grid of images grouped into sections: colour palettes, patterns, standout
graphics, typography and so on.

Along the top are filter buttons:

- **Tier** — *Curated* is the hand-picked half, held to a quality standard. *Product sweep*
  is real products from other brands, shown unfiltered so you can see the field.
- **Territory** — the design worlds: Neo-Japanese, Cyberpunk, Streetwear, Printmaking,
  Motorsport, Editorial.
- **Lane** — where it came from: motocross, BJJ competitors, reference artwork.

Under each image is a short note explaining *why* it's there — the design idea to take
from it, not just what it is.

### 2. Mark it

On every card:

- **☆ Star** — this is good, I want more like it
- **✕ Reject** — this isn't right, then pick a reason from the list

**The reasons matter more than the stars.** "Too plain", "looks cheap", "too busy",
"seen everywhere" — each one is a specific instruction. Reject three things for the same
reason and it becomes a permanent rule: that kind of thing stops appearing.

Being harsh is useful. A board where you reject twenty things and keep three teaches it
far more than one where you shrug at everything.

### 3. Send your review back

When you're done, press **Copy** at the top. A box appears with your picks in it,
already copied to your clipboard.

**Paste that box into Slack or email and send it back.** That's what feeds it into the
system.

---

## Two people reviewing

Both of you can review independently — at different times, on different computers.

Once both reviews are in, each card shows what the other person thought: a gold
**★ James** on what he kept, a red **✕ James — too plain** on what he rejected, and a
filter button to see only his picks.

Where you **both** starred something, that's the strongest signal there is, and it gets
weighted above either of you alone.

Where you **disagree** — one kept it, one rejected it — nothing happens automatically.
It gets flagged for the two of you to talk about, because quietly turning a disagreement
into a rule would lock in one person's taste without anyone noticing.

---

## Saving — the one-time passphrase

The very first time you star something, you'll be asked for a **team passphrase**. Type it
once and that device remembers it forever. Ask Ladana for it.

After that, saving is automatic. You'll see **saved** with a timestamp at the top whenever
your marks go through. There's no save button to remember.

Because your marks live on the internet rather than in one browser:

- Star things on your laptop, open it on your phone later — they're all there ✓
- Both of you see each other's marks without sending anything ✓
- Clearing your browsing history doesn't lose your work ✓

If you're somewhere with no signal it still works — it saves locally and pushes everything
up the moment you're back online. The status at the top tells you which is happening.

## Worth knowing

**The board updates when a new research run happens**, not continuously. It's a weekly
report you can mark up, not a live feed.

**Everything on it is other people's work**, shown as reference with a link to the source.
The notes describe the *technique* worth borrowing. It's a research tool, not a copying tool.

---

## If something looks wrong

**The page is blank or images are missing.** Refresh once. If it's still empty, you may have
been sent the *file* rather than the *link* — always use the link at the top of this page.

**It looks out of date.** Refresh. Your browser may be showing a saved copy.

**It says "wrong passphrase".** Click a star again and you'll be asked to re-enter it.

**It says "offline — saved in this browser only".** Your marks are safe; they'll sync as
soon as you're back on a connection.

---

## The short version

1. Open the link
2. Star the good, reject the bad **with a reason**
3. Press **Copy** and send the box back

That's the whole job. Everything else happens behind it.
